Every assistant you use starts each conversation from zero. You paste the
context back in. You re-explain the project, the people, the decision you made
on Tuesday. Then you copy the answer out and do the work yourself.

That is not an assistant. That is a very articulate blank page.

ShogunAI is the operating system for the AI-native individual. It has two
layers, and the second one is the point.

## The memory layer

ShogunAI builds a model of your work while you work. Not a folder of notes you
have to maintain — a running picture of what you read, wrote, and decided,
assembled passively in the background and stored on your Mac.

It captures text through the accessibility layer macOS already exposes to
screen readers. It is not a screen recorder. There are no screenshots sitting
in a folder, no video timeline, no audio files. What gets stored is text and
where it came from.

That distinction is the whole design, and we wrote more about it in
[Passive memory, not screenshots](/blog/passive-memory-not-screenshots).

## The execution layer

Memory that only answers questions is a search box with better manners. The
reason to know what happened in your day is to finish what it left open.

So ShogunAI reads its own memory and does the work: drafts the follow-up you
said you would send, prepares the brief for the call you have in an hour,
files the thing you forgot to file. It reaches your tools through their own
APIs, and anything that leaves your machine — a sent email, a posted message,
a created event — stops for your explicit approval first.

We wrote about where those gates sit in
[The execution layer](/blog/the-execution-layer).

## Why both, why now

Three things became true at roughly the same time. Models got good enough to
act on messy real-world context instead of just summarizing it. Tools grew a
common protocol layer, so an agent can reach twenty of them without twenty
bespoke integrations. And laptops got fast enough to run capture, indexing,
and search locally without you noticing.

In 2007 the iPhone folded the phone, the camera, the browser, and the music
player into one device. Memory, execution, and data sovereignty are having
that moment now. They arrive as separate products today. They belong in one
system.

## What we will not do

Some constraints are worth stating before we ship anything, because they are
the parts that get quietly traded away later:

- **No screenshots, no recordings.** Text and provenance, not pixels and
  waveforms. The one narrow exception is opt-in and time-boxed, and we
  [explain it here](/blog/visual-recall).
- **Your machine is the default.** Memory lives locally. What goes out goes
  out for a named reason, and the log tells you which reason.
- **Nothing sends itself.** Automatic actions are the reversible, local ones.
  Anything another human will see waits for you.
- **No free tier funded by your data.** Every plan is a paid plan, starting
  with a seven-day full trial. You are the customer, which is the only
  arrangement that keeps the above three honest.

ShogunAI is in private beta on macOS. If your day is full of context that
nobody but you is holding, it was built for you.
