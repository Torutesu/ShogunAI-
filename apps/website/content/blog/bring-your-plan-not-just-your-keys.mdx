Until now, using the execution layer meant pasting an API key. That is a fine
ask for engineers and a strange one for everybody else — a second billing
relationship, a console to visit, a spending limit to guess at, all to use a
model you are already paying a monthly subscription for.

So we changed the default. **Pro now runs on the assistant plan you already
have.** An API key still works, and for some people it is the better option.
It is no longer the price of entry.

## How it works

If you already use one of the major assistant subscriptions and have its
official command-line tool installed and signed in, ShogunAI can run agent work
through it. You opt in explicitly, once, in settings. From then on drafting,
reasoning, and tool use happen inside the plan you are already paying for, at
no additional per-token cost to you.

Two things we do not do, and will not:

- **We do not read other applications' credential stores.** No rummaging
  through another app's keychain entries or config files. ShogunAI launches the
  vendor's own tool, the way you would in a terminal, and that tool handles its
  own authentication.
- **We do not reimplement anyone's consumer sign-in.** Every request runs
  through the vendor's official client, under their terms, with their rate
  limits. That is the only version of this that is safe for your account.

If the tool is not installed or not signed in, ShogunAI says so and falls back
to a key.

## Why the two lanes stay separate

There is a second kind of model work in ShogunAI that never touches your
credentials: indexing, classification, the nightly review, and the morning
brief. That work is batched, runs on our infrastructure, and is covered by your
subscription. It is the reason Standard needs no key at all.

Keeping the lanes separate is not bookkeeping. Batch work is high-volume by
nature, and pointing it at a personal monthly allowance would burn through the
allowance in days — leaving your agent dead until the plan resets. So the split
is enforced: our capacity does the bulk work, your credentials do the work you
asked for by name.

## What this changes about the price

Pro is $99 a month billed annually. Before this change, the honest sticker
price included whatever your API usage came to on top — which for heavy users
was not a rounding error. Now, for most people, it is the sticker price, and
the model spend is one you had already decided to make.

Standard is $49 and needs no credentials at all: capture, recall, search, the
notch, connected tools for reading context, the nightly review, and the morning
brief. Both plans start with a seven-day full trial.

## The point underneath

This is the same argument as local-first, one layer up. Your memory should not
be locked to a vendor, and neither should your inference. Bring the plan you
already have, swap it later when a better one exists, and keep every day of
context you have accumulated in the meantime.
