## Memory should follow the work, not record a surveillance feed

Screenshots are a blunt proxy for context. They collect visual frames without explaining what mattered, what changed, or what should happen next. Passive memory should be selective, searchable, and controlled by the person doing the work.

ShogunAI is designed around local-first capture. Useful signals stay on the device by default, and connected AI providers receive only the context needed for an approved request. The goal is continuity without creating a permanent cloud copy of your day.

## What useful passive memory looks like

It should help you recover a decision, connect a conversation to a document, and prepare the next action. It should also make deletion, pausing, and permission boundaries obvious.

That is the difference between remembering work and watching a screen.
