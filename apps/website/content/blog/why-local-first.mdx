Local-first means the default boundary is the device you use for work. That boundary matters because a personal memory layer can contain decisions, relationships, private documents, and unfinished thoughts that were never meant to become a permanent cloud record.

## What local-first unlocks

Keeping capture and storage on-device reduces unnecessary copies, makes deletion meaningful, and lets people choose when a model provider receives relevant context. It also makes the privacy model easier to explain: local by default, shared by explicit action.

Local-first does not mean every optional AI request is offline. When you approve a connected provider, the necessary context may leave the device under that provider’s terms. The important part is that the choice is visible and yours.
